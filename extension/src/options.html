Answers employers ask for

Answer these once and the extension fills them on every form that asks. It never guesses: anything left blank here is left blank on the form and listed for you. Check them before you submit — these are answers given in your name.

Your own questions

For questions no list can predict — "Do you have experience with MT4?", "Is a fixed-term contract acceptable?". Put a few words from the question on the left and your answer on the right. The longest match wins, so a specific pair beats a vague one. Answers you save with Remember what I typed on a form appear here too.

Diversity questions

These are voluntary on every employer's form, and they stay blank here unless you fill them in. What you type is stored in this browser only and is never sent anywhere. Leaving them blank means the extension leaves every such question for you.

Job-site accounts

Workday, UKG and Oracle make you create an account before you can apply. The extension fills the email and this password into their "Create account" and "Sign in" pages, including the "verify password" box. You press Create Account or Sign In yourself.

Most of these sites require at least 8 characters with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter, a number and a symbol. Use one you do not use for email or banking: the same password goes to every company's account. Stored in this browser only, and included in "Download a backup" — keep that file private.
